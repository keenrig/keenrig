# Security policy

Please **do not** report security problems in public issues.

Email **hello@keenrig.com** with the subject `SECURITY` and include:

- the affected component (keenrig-box, installer, catalog) and version;
- steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept;
- the impact you expect.

We aim to reply within 3 working days and will credit you in the release notes unless you
ask us not to.

## Known limitation

Until the owner account is created, the first person to open the Admin at `http://<server-ip>/`
can create it. Create the owner right after installing; the installer and the marketplace
first-boot script print this reminder.
